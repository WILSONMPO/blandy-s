Meet Blandina.

Writer · Mindset Coach · Podcaster

I write essays and record conversations about the architecture of the mind — how we build resilience, rewire habits, and reclaim our sense of self in a noisy world.

Blandina Mashala is a writer and host of the Mind Over Matter podcast, exploring the psychology of everyday life. Her essays sit at the intersection of neuroscience, quiet spirituality, and the messy business of being human. She lives, writes, and drinks too much coffee somewhere warm.
